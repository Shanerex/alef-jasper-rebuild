Our Story

Welcome to Alef

مرحباً بكم في ألف للخدمات المعمارية والكادية

In view of the boom and vast growth in the Construction Industry, Alef Architectural & Cadding Services L.L.C. was established in March 2003.

Since then the company has been providing premium quality services such as Architectural Prospective Drawings, Structural General Arrangement, Co-ordinates, MEP, Rebar Shop Drawings, Bar Bending Schedules, As-Built drawings and Quantity Surveying. Alef maintains its Indian Corporate Back Office, M/s Jasper International Engineering Consultants, at Tirunelveli District, Tamil Nadu — South India — with facilities to employ 246 staff.
