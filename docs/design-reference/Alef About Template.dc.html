About Alef

من نحن

Over two decades of engineering excellence, delivering premium structural and cadding services to the Gulf's most ambitious construction projects.

Established 2003

Two Decades of
Engineering Excellence

"In view of the boom and vast growth in the Construction Industry, Alef Architectural & Cadding Services L.L.C. was established in March 2003."

Since then the company has been providing premium quality services such as preparing Architectural Prospective Drawings, Structural General Arrangement, Co-ordinate Drawings, MEP, Rebar Shop Drawings, Bar Bending Schedules, As-Built drawings and Quantity Surveying.

Alef has its Indian Back-Of Corporate Office M/s. Jasper International Engineering Consultants at Vasudevanallur of Tirunelveli District, Tamil Nadu in South India — with facilities to employ 246 staff, fully functional with a large Work-Station and Quality Control department.
