Rebar Shop Drawings
with Bar Bending Schedules

Alef specializes in the preparation of detailed rebar shop drawings complete with Bar Bending Schedules (BBS). Our drawings are prepared in strict accordance with project specifications and applicable codes — ensuring accuracy from reinforcement placement to on-site execution.

Each BBS is carefully checked by our Quality Control team before submission, minimizing RFI cycles and reducing on-site waste.

MEP Drawings

Alef prepares coordinated Mechanical, Electrical and Plumbing (MEP) drawings for building services across all disciplines. Our drawings are produced to facilitate smooth installation, avoiding costly clashes on site.

We work across HVAC, plumbing, drainage, fire-fighting, low-voltage and power systems — co-ordinating all services within the architectural envelope with precision.
