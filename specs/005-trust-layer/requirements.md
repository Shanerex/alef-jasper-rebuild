# Requirements: Trust Layer

## Problem
Capacity and credibility are buried in About prose. Surface them where buyers decide.

## User stories
- As a buyer, I see the scale and track record at a glance.
- As a buyer, I recognize the calibre of past clients and contractors.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] F5-AC1 Home shows years in business, staff count, monthly steel capacity, marquee project count.
- [ ] F5-AC2 Client/contractor names shown as a logo or named strip.
- [ ] F5-AC3 Software and standards (AutoCAD, CADS RC, SteelPac RC, international detailing standards) shown as capability badges.

## Out of scope
- Testimonials engine (later).
