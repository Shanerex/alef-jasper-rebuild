# Requirements: Client Portal (stretch)

## Problem
Active clients want to track drawing submittals and RFIs. This is where a real backend earns its place. Specced lightly until the first five ship.

## User stories
- As an active client, I log in and see my project's submittals.
- As an active client, I track RFIs and their status.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] F6-AC1 Authenticated access scoped to a client's own projects.
- [ ] F6-AC2 Submittal tracking per project.
- [ ] F6-AC3 RFI log with status.

## Out of scope (v1 of portal)
- Billing, document e-signing.

## Note
Stretch. Do not start before 001-005 are green.
